### Overview

Sets the auto-kerning method of the selected text to Japanese monospaced (metrics for Roman only).
Japanese monospaced = `METRICSROMANONLY`. Proportional metrics is off and tsume is 0%.
It applies to the whole selection immediately, and does nothing when nothing is selected.
