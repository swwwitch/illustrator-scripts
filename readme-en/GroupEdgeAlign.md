### Overview

Aligns the edges or the center of the selected objects to the edge or the center of the active artboard, or to a matching guide.

### Features

- The direction is picked on a 3×3 grid of radio buttons
- The direction can also be derived from the filename
- Snapping to guides via `USE_GUIDES` and `GUIDE_SEARCH_MODE`

### Usage

1. Select the objects to align.
2. Run the script.
3. Choose the direction and run it.

### Notes

- The seven scripts under `GroupEdgeAlign-7/` cover the individual directions without a dialog.
